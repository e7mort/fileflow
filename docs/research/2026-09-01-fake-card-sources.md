## Database growth plan

1. Add every new card-specific guide as a `CardProfile` with linked telltale IDs.
2. Invite researcher submissions (photo macros + which tell failed).
3. Later: train CV models on labeled real/fake crops for fonts/energy icons — still keep explainable checklist as UX core.
4. Prefer community attribution; never scrape private Discord without permission.

## Pricing thesis

Competitors charge service-tier prices for human/AI auth. HoloCheck undercuts with **$1/report** and **$5/10 checks** so impulse verification before a purchase is frictionless.
